How to reach us

Phone is the only way to book. There is no online form, because a leak or a backup should not wait in an inbox. Call, describe the problem, and dispatch arranges the visit.

Our office is at 258 Main St, Milford, MA 01757. This is a dispatch and business address, so please call before driving over, since the plumbers are usually out on jobs.

What to tell dispatch when you call

A few details help us send the right plumber with the right parts on the first trip.

Have these ready if you can:

  • Your street address and town.
  • What is leaking, blocked or not working, and where in the house.
  • Whether water is near electrical equipment or the problem is spreading.
  • Whether you have found and closed the main shutoff valve.
  • The age and fuel of a water heater or boiler, if that is the problem.

Emergencies: do not wait for business hours

If water is flowing and will not stop, sewage is coming up a drain or a pipe has burst, call now. According to the state's consumer guidance, plumbing repairs on supply or drain piping need a licensed plumber. Dispatch treats these as emergencies at any hour, including nights, weekends and holidays.

While you wait, close the main water valve and keep people and pets away from standing water.

If you smell gas:

  • Leave the house right away and do not touch light switches.
  • Call your gas utility or 911 from outside.
  • Call us after the gas is made safe, for any plumbing work that follows.
