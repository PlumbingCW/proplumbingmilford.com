Private wells and septic systems: where our work starts and stops

The town says public water reaches only the Route 140 area and the part of Route 16 near the Hopedale line. Most residents rely on private wells. Mendon also has no town sewer, so each home has its own septic system. That changes what a plumbing call looks like here.

On the water side, we work on the pressure tank and its fittings, valves, house piping, filter plumbing and leaks. If the pump itself has failed down in the well, that is usually a well contractor's job, and we will say so on the phone.

On the waste side, we clear the house drain and sewer line out to the tank. We do not pump tanks or rebuild leaching fields. Those belong to a septic pumper or installer. We can tell you which side of the tank the problem is on.

Signs your private water system needs a plumber:

  • The pump kicks on and off every few seconds when one tap is running.
  • Pressure swings from strong to a trickle in the same shower.
  • A puddle or rust stain sits under the pressure tank.
  • Water turns cloudy or brown right after the pump starts.
  • Every drain in the house runs slow, not just one.

Emergency plumbing in Mendon at night or on weekends

On a well, a burst pipe keeps getting fed. The pump runs until the power is cut or the supply is closed, so a small break can flood a basement fast. Call the 24/7 line and a plumber can be dispatched that night or the same day.

Give dispatch your exact address and mention any gate, long drive or unplowed stretch, since the town cannot plow unaccepted roads.

While you wait for the plumber:

  • Close the main shutoff where the water line enters the house.
  • Turn off the well pump breaker if water is still flowing after the main is closed.
  • Do not touch outlets or the panel if water is dripping near them.
  • If the septic tank alarm is sounding, stop using water and tell dispatch.

What Mendon homeowners call us for, in order

Well and tank problems come first. Our plumbers test pressure and check the tank before replacing anything.

Second are drain backups that people fear are septic failure. A single blocked line is often a clog in the house pipe, and a drain cleaning visit can settle it. If every fixture is slow and a sewage smell rises near the yard, the tank or field is more likely, and a sewer camera inspection shows how far the house line is clear.

Third is hot water. Well water carries minerals that can shorten the life of a tank heater, so water heater repair comes up often. Frozen lines in a garage or crawl space come fourth, and a burst pipe repair visit starts with the shutoff.
