What do Holliston homeowners call us for?

Hot water tops the list. The town pumps groundwater from five supply wells, and its published report says iron and manganese are removed at some of them. Minerals that reach a heater settle as sediment on the bottom of the tank. A heater that rumbles, runs out early or leaks at the base is usually showing that age.

Our plumbers handle water heater repair first, from a failed element to a bad thermostat. If the tank itself is rusted through at the seam, repair is not the answer and we say so.

Next come basement calls. The Charles River runs near the south of town and Bogastow Brook crosses it, so houses on low ground depend on a sump pump that must work in a wet week. The sump pump repair page covers what to check before you call.

Why do older Holliston houses clog and leak more?

Holliston was once the largest shoe-producing town in the country, and the housing near the old village centers shows that age. Houses built decades ago may still have cast iron or galvanized pipe inside the walls, and both corrode from the inside until flow drops.

Slow drains in those houses are often scale, not just a clog. A drain cleaning visit clears the pipe, and a camera pass shows how much wall is left. If the pipe is thin, we explain whether repiping a section is worth it or whether cleaning will hold for now.

Signs the pipes themselves, not a clog, are the issue:

  • Rust-colored water from the cold tap first thing in the morning.
  • Low pressure at several fixtures while the street main is fine.
  • A pinhole spray or green crust on a pipe joint.
  • Drains that clear and slow down again within weeks.

Is my street on town sewer or septic?

We cannot answer that from the road, and no guess is safe. Ask the Holliston DPW whether your street is on town sewer. The answer changes who owns the pipe that backs up and where the blockage might sit.

If you are on a town line, a backup that hits only your house is usually in your own lateral. If you are on septic, a clog in the house branch is still ours to clear, but a full tank is a pumper's job. Either way, a sewer camera inspection shows which side you are dealing with.

Plumbing emergencies in Holliston at night

A pipe does not pick a convenient hour. A ceiling that drips at 2 a.m., a toilet that overflows on a holiday and a water heater leaking into a finished basement all get the same answer: call, and the line sends a plumber out whatever the hour.

The one thing we cannot do is work on a gas leak. If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility or 911 first. After the utility clears the house, we can handle the plumbing side.

While you wait:

  • Close the main valve and open one low tap to drain the lines.
  • Put a bucket under an active drip and move furniture away.
  • Do not use an electrical outlet in a wet room.
  • Keep children and pets out of any sewage.
